## About Me

<img src="https://github.com/jairao31/jairao31/blob/main/Assets/memoji.png" align="right" width="22%" style="margin-left: 20px"/>

Senior Full Stack Engineer with **6+ years** shipping products at scale — across insurtech, fintech, and enterprise SaaS. I build and architect full systems, not just features.

At **Afficiency**, I've been part of scaling a platform to **30,000+ agents** across 6-8 carriers processing **$Millions in annual premium** — while co-defining how the entire team ships software using AI-augmented development.

**What I'm known for:**

- 🤖 Pioneered a company-wide AI engineering SOP using **Claude Code** — standardized specs, BRDs, and custom skills as AI artifacts, cutting delivery from weeks to ~1 week with Playwright test coverage end-to-end
- 🏗️ Shipped **6+ major platform initiatives** in production: fraud detection (LexisNexis), e-signature platform, **Monitorly** (in-house LogRocket alternative, 40% tooling cost reduction), and **AffiHub** (in-house HubSpot-equivalent CRM)
- ⚡ Led a full mono-repo architecture overhaul across 6–8 carrier integrations and 2,000+ code reviews
- 🎓 **MS Computer Science**, Stevens Institute of Technology — GPA: 3.84, Provost's Scholarship
